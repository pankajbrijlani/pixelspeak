The story

Vael comes to DJing after twenty years in visual effects and photography, bringing the same eye for timing, colour and pacing to the decks. Every set is built like an edit: tension, release, and a finish people remember.

Based in Toronto and playing across the GTA, Vael is available for clubs, private events and brand nights.
