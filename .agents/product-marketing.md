## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Creatives are paid to shoot, but editing eats most of their week — and it doesn't scale.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Offshore editing farms: cheap but inconsistent, style drift, communication friction, revisions
- AI auto-edit tools: fast, but generic and weak on hard fixes
- Doing it yourself: burnout, slow deliveries, fewer bookings
- Hiring an in-house editor: fixed cost for seasonal work
**What it costs them:** Lost bookings, slow delivery reviews, evenings and weekends, burnout.
**Emotional tension:** Fear of handing their "look" to someone who'll ruin it; guilt about late galleries.
